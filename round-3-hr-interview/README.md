# Round 3 — HR Interview

[← Back to main README](../README.md)

> **Reported format:** 10–20 minutes. Checks **communication, attitude, culture fit**, what you know about **Capgemini**, and logistics (relocation, shifts).

---

## 1. "Tell me about yourself" (60–90 seconds)

**Formula: Present → Past → Future**

> *"I'm Ananya, in my final year of MCA at Presidency University, Bengaluru. I enjoy backend development and I've recently been working with AI tools in my projects.*
> *In my main project I built a lab-booking system in Spring Boot and MySQL that's now used by three labs, and I cleared the HackerRank Java certification.*
> *I'm applying to Capgemini because I want to start my career working on real client projects at scale, and the Exceller program's focus on AI-ready engineers matches exactly what I've been building towards."*

❌ Don't recite your resume line by line. ❌ Don't start with family details unless asked.

---

## 2. Common questions, and how to answer

| Question | Framework / tip |
|---|---|
| **Why Capgemini?** | One fact about the company + one link to you. *"Capgemini works with clients across 50+ countries and is investing heavily in AI; its new assessment itself tests AI skills. I want to grow in exactly that environment."* |
| **What do you know about us?** | Use the facts below. Mention one recent thing. |
| **Strengths?** | Pick 1–2, **each with proof**. *"I learn fast: I picked up Spring Boot in 2 weeks for my project."* |
| **Weakness?** | A real one + what you're doing about it. *"I used to hesitate to speak up in groups; I've been presenting in class seminars to work on it."* |
| **Where do you see yourself in 5 years?** | Growth *inside* the company. *"A strong engineer leading a small module, with a cloud or AI certification."* |
| **A challenge you faced?** | Use **STAR** (below). |
| **Are you okay with relocation / shifts?** | Be honest. If yes, say so confidently: *"Yes, I'm flexible. I see it as part of starting my career."* |
| **Do you plan higher studies?** | *"Not in the near future. I want industry experience first."* (If true!) |
| **Any questions for us?** | **Always ask one** (see below). |

## 3. STAR method (for any "tell me about a time…")

| | Meaning | Share of your answer |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ituation | Context, briefly | 10% |
| **T**ask | What you had to do | 10% |
| **A**ction | What **you** did (say "I") | 60% |
| **R**esult | The outcome, a number if possible, + what you learned | 20% |

> *S:* Two days before our project demo, our database crashed. *T:* I was responsible for the backend. *A:* I restored from the last Git commit, rewrote the migration scripts and set up daily backups. *R:* We demoed on time, scored an A, and I now always automate backups.

Prepare **3 STAR stories**: a challenge, a teamwork moment, and a time you learned something fast.

## 4. Capgemini facts to know

> ⚠️ Verify the "current" facts on [capgemini.com](https://www.capgemini.com/in-en/) the week of your interview; leadership and figures change.

- **Founded:** 1967 in Grenoble, France, by **Serge Kampf**
- **Headquarters:** Paris, France
- **Business:** IT services, consulting, technology and digital transformation, engineering, and now a big focus on **AI and generative AI**
- **India:** Capgemini's largest workforce: almost **180,000 employees across 13 locations**, including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad, Noida and Gurugram
- **India CEO:** **Sanjay Chalke** (took over from Ashwin Yardi on 1 January 2026, as announced in October 2025)
- **7 values:** **Honesty, Boldness, Trust, Freedom, Team Spirit, Modesty, Fun**. Map one of your STAR stories to one value.
- **Exceller:** Capgemini's campus program for fresh technical graduates. The new 2026 assessment tests AI literacy and AI-assisted coding, which shows Capgemini wants an **"AI-ready" workforce**.
- **Fresher roles:** Analyst / Senior Analyst (tiers are reported to depend on assessment performance)

## 5. Questions to ask the interviewer
- *"What does the first 90 days look like for a new Analyst?"*
- *"Which technologies do freshers in this role usually work on?"*
- *"How does Capgemini support learning, for example certifications in cloud or AI?"*

## ❌ Pitfalls
- Badmouthing your college, teachers or classmates
- Saying "I'll leave for higher studies soon"
- Strengths with no example
- Not knowing what Capgemini does
- One-word answers, or talking for 5 minutes non-stop
