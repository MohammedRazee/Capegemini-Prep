# Stage 5 — Cognitive Assessment

[← Back to stages](../README.md)

> Four modules, each measuring something different. They're **game-based**, so practice makes a big difference: your first attempt is always your worst.

| Module | Time | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion Challenge** | 6 min | Complex planning & problem solving |
| **Grid Challenge** | 12 min | Working memory & spatial attention |
| **Logical Reasoning** | ~25 min | Logic, decision-making, analysing problems, systems thinking |
| **Behavioural Module** | 25 min | Adaptive personality assessment |

---

## 🎮 Motion Challenge (6 min)

**What it is:** A sliding-block puzzle. Move blocks around a grid so a **red ball reaches the hole**, in as **few moves as possible**. Puzzles get harder as you solve them.

**Strategy:**
- **Plan backwards from the hole.** Ask "what's blocking the path?", then "what's blocking *that* block?"
- **Count moves before you touch anything.** Fewer moves scores better than speed.
- Don't get stuck. If a puzzle has a skip or reset option and you're lost after ~45 seconds, use it.
- Small puzzles first in practice; build the "see 3 moves ahead" habit.

## 🎮 Grid Challenge (12 min)

**What it is:** Dots appear one at a time on a grid. Between them you do a quick **distraction task** (e.g., judging whether a pattern is symmetrical). At the end you tap where the dots appeared, **in order**.

**Strategy:**
- **Give each position a name** as it appears: "top-left", "row 2 col 3". Saying it silently in words helps you remember.
- Group dots into a **shape** (an L, a diagonal) when you can.
- Don't rush the distraction task. Accuracy on it counts too.
- Sleep well the night before. Working memory drops sharply when you're tired.

## 🎮 Possibly also: Switch & Digit Challenges

Prep sites say Capgemini sometimes draws other games from a pool. Only Motion and Grid are confirmed for Exceller, but a quick look at these is cheap insurance:
- **Switch Challenge:** work out which operator changed the order of symbols.
- **Digit Challenge:** fill in digits to make an equation true.

**Typical question types:** number and letter series, coding-decoding, blood relations, seating arrangements, syllogisms, statement and conclusion, direction sense, odd one out, pattern/figure series.

## 🙂 Behavioural Module (25 min)

An **adaptive personality assessment**. There are no "right" answers, but there are **inconsistent** ones.

- **Be honest and consistent.** The test asks similar things in different ways and detects faking.
- It helps to genuinely reflect on Capgemini's values: **teamwork, ownership, honesty, willingness to learn, adaptability.**
- Don't pick extremes on every question just to look "ideal".
- Read each statement fully; some are phrased negatively.
- Don't overthink. Your first instinct is usually your most consistent answer.
