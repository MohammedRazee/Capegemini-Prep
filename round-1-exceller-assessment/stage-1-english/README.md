# Stage 1 — English Communication

[← Back to stages](../README.md)

> **Tests:** Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing. **Eliminatory.** Speaking is scored **by a machine** on pronunciation, fluency and clarity. You don't need a perfect accent; you need **steady, clear, complete sentences without long pauses**.

---

## Task types you'll likely see (Versant-style)

| Task | What you do | How to score well |
|---|---|---|
| **Read aloud** | Read sentences on screen | Steady pace, don't rush, pronounce word endings |
| **Repeat** | Hear a sentence and repeat it exactly | Focus on the *whole* sentence; keep the rhythm even if you miss a word |
| **Short answer** | Answer a simple question in a word or two | Answer immediately; don't overthink |
| **Sentence builds** | Rearrange word groups into a sentence | Listen for subject → verb → object |
| **Retell / story** | Hear a short passage and retell it | Who, what, what happened, ending. Use the speaker's key words. |
| **Open question** | Give your opinion | Opinion → reason → example → conclusion (30–40 s) |
| **Fill in blanks / comprehension** | Reading questions | Read the question first, then scan the passage |
| **Email / passage writing** | Write a short email or passage | Greeting → purpose → details → polite close. Check grammar. |

## 🧪 15-minute daily drill

1. **Read aloud (3 min):** a news paragraph. Record it, then play it back.
2. **Repeat (4 min):** play 10 sentences from a podcast or news clip, pause after each, and repeat it word for word.
3. **Retell (4 min):** listen to a 30-second clip, then retell it in your own words in 30 seconds.
4. **Write (4 min):** a 5-line email, e.g. *"Write to your manager asking for leave on Friday."*

### Email template
```
Subject: Leave request for Friday, 16 October

Dear Ms. Sharma,

I would like to request a day of leave on Friday, 16 October, to attend a family function.
I have completed my pending tasks and Rahul has agreed to cover any urgent work.

Please let me know if this is acceptable.

Regards,
Arjun
```

## ❌ Pitfalls
- **Long silences.** The machine scores a pause as a failure. Start speaking within 1–2 seconds.
- **Talking too fast.** Fluency means *smooth*, not *fast*.
- **Starting before the beep**, or stopping early.
- **A noisy room or laptop mic.** Use a headset.
- **Correcting yourself repeatedly.** Keep going; one slip is better than a restart.
